## Tokens do not have to start as words

A transformer only receives vectors with positions and a compatible hidden dimension. An image encoder can turn patches into vectors; an audio encoder can turn frames into vectors; a projection layer can map those vectors into the language model’s representation space. The transformer then processes a mixed sequence.

## Alignment is learned

Putting vectors in the same dimension does not guarantee that “cat” and a cat image are nearby or that a visual patch can answer a textual question. Contrastive objectives, paired data, instruction tuning, and cross-modal adapters teach the model how the modalities relate.

## Sequence design is an architectural choice

A multimodal system may concatenate all tokens, use cross-attention, or route modalities through separate towers before fusion. Each choice changes memory, position handling, and where information can flow. Use the embedding plane to distinguish shared coordinates from shared meaning.

The central idea is **Multimodal transformers**.
